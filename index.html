Come Curious, Leave Connected

Explore all our hobby categories designed to spark creativity, connection, and discovery.

Arts & Design

Explore hobbies rooted in aesthetics, visual storytelling, and personal expression through drawing, painting, graphic design, or style-focused disciplines.

Collecting & Curating

Celebrate the joy of preserving, organizing, and showcasing meaningful objects, whether they hold historical, cultural, or nostalgic value.

DIY & Crafting

Channel your drive to make things by hand. This category focuses on at-home projects, repair culture, and the pride of building something yourself.

Food & Beverage

Where flavor meets fascination. Whether you’re experimenting in the kitchen or seeking finer techniques, this space celebrates edible hobbies of all kinds.

Games & Puzzles

Dive into interactive worlds and brain-teasing challenges. This category includes analog and digital play, logic, storytelling, and strategy.

Performance & Stage

Designed for expressive, theatrical, or audience-based hobbies. Whether you’re performing or producing, this is a space for spotlight moments and audience connection.

Science, Tech & Tinkering

This is where experimentation, invention, and technical curiosity come to life. Whether analog or digital, it’s about asking “what if?” and diving into the how.

Sports & Movement

Where bodies are in motion for fun, fitness, or focus. Whether competitive or meditative, movement-based hobbies belong here.

Volunteering & Service

For those who turn hobbies into impact. This category supports community-minded efforts, skill-sharing, and acts of care and contribution.

A community, not just an event

HobbyCon is building year-round connection: hobby groups, local meetups, and ongoing discovery.
