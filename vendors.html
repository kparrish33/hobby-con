Vendor Application

Welcome to the HobbyCon Vendor Application! We are so excited to welcome a variety of hobbies to our event. Applicants can expect to hear back 5-7 days after submission. If you have any questions or find any technical difficulties with this form, email info@hobbycon.com for assistance.

Welcome to HobbyCon

HobbyCon is a one-of-a-kind gathering of learners and doers, where curiosity sparks discovery, passions are cultivated through practice, and communities form around shared interests. We aim to build a vibrant ecosystem of exploration and inclusiveness for every hobbyist.

What to Expect

As a vendor you choose how to experience HobbyCon! We offer spots to table (price varies for vendor types) and opportunities to do more!

Exhibitor

Vendors who plan to table can expect a 10x10 space & table in the HobbyCon exhibit hall and 2 free exhibitor badges. In order to encourage our small clubs and local groups, pricing will vary:

  • Community Organizations/Clubs/Non-profits $50/day
  • Businesses $200/day
  • Caterers $300/day
  • Extra space, chairs, electricity, additional badges, etc. will be available at an additional cost

Workshop

Workshops are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. While hosting a workshop with HobbyCon is a great opportunity to advertise to new and existing hobbyists, we understand the costs that come with providing the tools, expertise, and time of a workshop. We're excited to hear your ideas and help bring them to life! With prior notice, assistance may be provided by HobbyCon but is not guaranteed.
