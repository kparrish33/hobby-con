What is HobbyCon?

HobbyCon is a welcoming space to explore hobbies with zero pressure. Come curious, leave connected — and maybe with a brand new interest you didn’t expect.

Beginner-safe

Designed for people trying something new — friendly intros, guided experiences, and no judgment.

Community-first

Meet people who share your curiosity — from casual dabblers to dedicated hobbyists.

Creative & expressive

A spectrum of hobbies — art, making, movement, games, tinkering — all in one place.

Modern, not corporate

Polished and organized — but warm, playful, and human.

The HobbyCon experience

Try new hobbies through hands-on sessions, quick-start workshops, demos, and meetups. You’re not expected to “already know” anything.

FAQ

Do I need experience to attend?

Nope. HobbyCon is designed for beginners. You can explore and try things without pressure.

Is HobbyCon only once a year?

The big event is annual, but the community and programming can run year-round.

What kinds of hobbies are included?

A wide range — creative, games, DIY, outdoors, performance, sports & movement, tech & tinkering, and more.

How do I become a vendor or instructor?

Start with the Exhibitor/Vendor option above (or add a dedicated application page later).
